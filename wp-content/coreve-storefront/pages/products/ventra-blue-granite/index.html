The design

Leather upper. TPR sole. Lace-up closure. Made in India. The wedge construction has a 65mm heel and 25mm forefoot. Designed as a fashion sneaker for everyday wear.

Delivery & returns

Free standard shipping across India. Processing: 1–2 business days. Transit after dispatch: metros 2–5, other cities and towns 4–7, remote areas 7–10 business days. Eligible unused pairs can be returned or exchanged within 7 days in original packaging. Confirm return shipping charges with support.
