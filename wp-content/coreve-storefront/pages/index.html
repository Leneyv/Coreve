Before you choose your pair

Delivery, fit and care.

Find your size

The collection is available in EU 37–41, equivalent to UK / India sizes 4–8. Measure your foot and consult the size guide. Between sizes? Coreve recommends sizing up. .

Delivery across India

Standard shipping is free. Ready-made orders process in 1–2 business days; transit after dispatch takes 2–5 business days in metros, 4–7 in other cities and towns, and 7–10 in remote areas.

Returns and exchanges

Eligible ready-made pairs can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging with tags intact. Contact Coreve to confirm the process and any return shipping charges.

The Queens Club Refab benefit

Register your sneaker’s serial number as a Queens Club member to request one complimentary upper-and-sole restoration between 6 and 12 months after purchase. Registration is required.
